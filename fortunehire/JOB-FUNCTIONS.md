# Responsibility-based job functions

During the existing official-page refresh, the collector keeps at most 16 short action-oriented responsibility sentences and a structured employer category. It uses only the matched posting's description, never the surrounding career page. Qualifications, benefits and company sections are excluded. No extra fetches, paid services or model calls are added.

The shared browser classifier uses specific action/object phrases to recognize duties. Repeated evidence of core work outweighs single ancillary tasks; ties can produce multiple functions. Recognized responsibilities take precedence over category and title. If responsibilities are insufficient, an explicit employer category supports classification, then the existing title rules serve as fallback. Details disclose the basis and the supporting excerpts. This is rule-based classification and may still miss unfamiliar wording; it does not claim employer-verified categories.

Existing records receive evidence when their normal check succeeds. Failed checks retain the previous job and evidence. Old personal-list snapshots without evidence continue to use titles; current records take precedence. Collection limits, posting-date and eligibility policies remain in place, including RTX's pause.

Verification includes every function, misleading titles, incidental terms, competing duties, category/title fallbacks, excluded sections, bounded evidence, refresh replacement, and filter integration. An offline spot check of 11 previously downloaded official descriptions found 8 with usable duty evidence; those 8 matched responsibility rules. Those historical samples were not republished or treated as current listings.
